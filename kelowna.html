Core Essence

Kelowna Hotel and Residences

Kelowna, Canada
Role: Designer
Concept

This concept takes its cues from the vineyards of the Okanagan Valley, the roadside fruit stands, the local farms and orchards that dot the region, and the bounties they produce. At the heart of the spa will live an interactive spa counter, a place of gathering and a complete immersion for the senses. Guests will be welcomed by the vibrant scents of fresh fruits, flowers, and herbs and invited to curate their own treatment elixirs amongst the laughter and joy of friends, both new and old.
