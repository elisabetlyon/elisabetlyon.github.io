hi

I've always been sensitive to the energy of a space. Even as a child, I was very aware of how different environments made me feel. Some made me feel calm and invited me to stay, while others left me feeling uneasy, with an instinctive desire to leave. Long before I had the words to explain it, I found myself rearranging the large bedroom I shared with my two sisters, intuitively trying to create a greater sense of balance and calm for us to enjoy. Even then, I was aware of how the energy of our space affected each of us in different ways.

As I grew older, I came to appreciate the beauty of a space: its colors, light, furniture, art, textures, materials, and finishes. Yet what mattered most to me was always how the space made me feel, no matter how beautiful it looked on the surface. Over time, I learned how much the places we live in reveal about our lives and how they can either support a healthy, harmonious, and balanced life or quietly work against it. This, combined with the fact that we spend 90% of our time indoors, makes that influence almost impossible to escape.

Today, that understanding guides my work as an interior designer across hospitality and residential projects. Bringing a sense of wellbeing into the spaces where people live will always be what drives me as a designer.

Let's work together to make your project feel like home.
