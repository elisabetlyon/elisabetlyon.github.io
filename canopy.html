Edge of Architecture (EOA)

Canopy by Hilton

Washington, DC
Role: Designer
Concept

"We Are One... This is Us"
Scott Circle, at the intersection of Rhode Island and Massachusetts Avenue, provided the perfect location for Canopy to come to life. The neighborhood, infused by multiple embassies and education centers, is the backdrop for a diverse, passionate, and politically enlightened demographic. This demographic is highly focused on intellectual efforts, on varying degrees of cultural formality, yet also enjoys simple, unencumbered, unpretentious social interaction. Canopy will be driven by a sense of welcoming: it will aim to embrace cultural diversity, to erase all boundaries, and to celebrate our togetherness as one species.
