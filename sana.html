SANA Holistic Center

Miami, FL
Concept

SANA is a holistic care center focused on wellness living, designed for adults navigating high stress and burnout. The goal is a space that helps guests unplug from the outside world and supports their healing process, achieved through interior and exterior spaces that incorporate nature as part of the architecture. Located in Miami, FL, the design aligns natural elements to enhance balance and harmony throughout the space.

The Alignment of the 7 Chakras
We live in an ever-changing world that moves faster than we do, and many of us have learned to cope with constant overwhelm. What we're really seeking is a different pace, one slow enough to hold our center and find inner peace and balance.

Healing is the body's search for balance. When we're in balance, our natural elements unify into one alignment, from our grounded root to the connection beyond the intangible, where healing is found.

The Holistic Center evokes the energy of the Chakras, revealing their elements and translating them into the function of each space.
