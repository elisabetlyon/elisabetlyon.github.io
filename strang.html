Elbow Cay Residence

Bahamas
Role: Designer
Concept

A bohemian style, expressed through a curated selection of unique and sustainable furniture, transforms this beach house into a beautiful sanctuary in the middle of the Atlantic Ocean.

Located in the Bahamas, the home blends modern design with local Caribbean influences. Rather than copying traditional styles, it uses them as inspiration. Designed to connect its inhabitants with the island's natural beauty, it offers sea views, cross-breezes, and open spaces.
